07 — Admissions

Entry 2027 open

Four steps
to Ashgrove.

No hoops, no pressure. Come and see the place, meet the people, and take it from there.

  1. Step 01

    Visit us

    Join an open morning or a private tour. Wander the studios, sit in on a lesson, talk to pupils.

  2. Step 02

    Apply

    A short form, a school report and a conversation. We are looking for curiosity, not perfection.

  3. Step 03

    Spend a day

    Your child comes in for a full day with their year group. It tells us more than any test.

  4. Step 04

    Join us

    Offers go out in January. Bursaries and scholarships are assessed alongside every application.
